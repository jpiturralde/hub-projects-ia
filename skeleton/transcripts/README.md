# Meeting transcripts

Primary **verbatim sources** for what was discussed in engagement meetings (internal, with {{CLIENT_DISPLAY_NAME}}, or mixed): rough notes, auto transcripts, tooling exports, etc.

## Role in the repo

- **Evidence / source of truth** for what was said in the session; they do not replace operational minutes or SPEC.
- **Operational version** (summary, decisions, action items, owners, next steps): [`backlog/meetings/`](../backlog/meetings/).
- **Formal documentation provided by the client**: [`docs/client-documentation/`](../docs/client-documentation/).

## Naming

- Lead with **date + topic** (e.g. `2026-05-06-workshop-architecture.md`).

## Editing and AI

- **Do not edit** unless the team explicitly agrees (repo rule `.cursor/rules/transcripts-immutable.mdc`).
- Agents read transcripts as **read-only context**; conclusions and follow-up live in `docs/`, `backlog/meetings/`, backlog tasks.
